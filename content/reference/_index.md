Detailed reference documentation for Froth.

The reference is split by responsibility:

- [Word Reference](/reference/words/) covers VM primitives and stdlib words.
- [Hardware APIs](/reference/hardware/) covers board and platform words such as `gpio.*`, `i2c.*`, `uart.*`, and `ms`.
- [FFI](/reference/ffi/) covers the Froth/C boundary and project-specific FFI declared in `froth.toml`.
- The remaining pages cover build configuration, tooling, and persistence.
